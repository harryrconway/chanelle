What I bring

Energy from the first welcome

A great host sets the tone for the whole event. I make every guest feel welcome and included, keep the program running on time, and bring the warmth and energy that turn an event into a celebration.

Bringing people together is what I love most; it’s why I started She’s Social. Before your event we’ll talk it through on a consultation call, so I know your event, your people and what matters most to you.

Events I host

  • Community and charity events

  • Conferences and summits
