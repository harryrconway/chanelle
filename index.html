About me

30%

The vision I was born with

I know what it’s like to be underestimated.

Born with 30% vision and legally blind in one eye, I grew up hearing what I couldn’t do. At 16 I was told I’d never drive. I believed every word, until I was housebound and at rock bottom.

So I challenged those beliefs. I took on a solo snow season in New Zealand, graduated with a Bachelor of Social Welfare and ran a half marathon. It was never my eye condition holding me back. It was my mindset.

Now I help audiences across Australia replace fear with belief, and step into a mindset that says “watch me”.
