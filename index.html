About me

Low
vision

What I was born with

I know what it’s like to be underestimated.

Born with low vision and legally blind in one eye, I grew up hearing what I couldn’t do. I believed every word, until I was housebound and at rock bottom.

So I challenged those beliefs, and found out what I was really capable of. Now I help audiences across Australia replace fear with belief.

What I do

I help audiences, brands, and communities connect with the power of belief, resilience, and possibility.

01

Talks on confidence, resilience and mindset, built on my own journey with low vision. Real stories and practical tools that help your audience replace fear with belief.

02

I bring energy and conviction to the stage, keeping your event flowing and your audience with you from the first welcome to the final word.

03

Honest, everyday stories about low vision, confidence and mindset, for my community and the brands that want to reach them.

04

Using my voice and my own experience of low vision to change how people see disability, and to help everyone feel safe, seen and included.
