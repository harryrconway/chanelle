What I do

I help audiences, brands, and communities connect with the power of belief, resilience, and possibility.

Book Chanelle to speak

01 · Motivational speaking

Replace fear with belief

Talks on confidence, resilience and mindset, built on my own journey with low vision. Real stories and practical tools that help your audience replace fear with belief and step into their full potential.

I speak to schools and youth leadership programs, community groups, disability and care organisations, and workplaces.

  • Living with disability
  • Inclusion and belonging
  • Resilience and mindset
  • Confidence building
  • Starting a business with disability

02 · Event hosting / MC

Energy from the first welcome

I bring energy and conviction to the stage, keeping your event flowing and your audience with you from the first welcome to the final word.

03 · She’s Social community

Connection, authenticity and fun

She’s Social is the women’s community I founded on the Gold Coast, bringing women together through genuine connection, fun events and uplifting friendships.

Weekly walks every Sunday at 7am and Wednesday at 5pm (QLD time) at Alia Cafe in Coolangatta, plus social nights and monthly events.

Follow @shessocial_

04 · Content creation

Honest, everyday stories

Honest, everyday stories about low vision, confidence and mindset, for my community and the brands that want to reach them. I’ve partnered with brands including TikTok, Petbarn, My Muscle Chef and Motel Rocks.
