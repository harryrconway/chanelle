Community

She’s Social

She’s Social is the women’s community I founded on the Gold Coast, bringing women together through genuine connection, fun events and uplifting friendships. Monthly walks on Sundays at 7am and Wednesdays at 5pm (QLD time) at Alia Cafe in Coolangatta, plus social nights and other events. I’m so grateful it’s grown into a community of over 2,000 followers.
