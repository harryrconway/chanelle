She's Social

She's Social is a women's community group located on the Gold Coast bringing women together through genuine connection, fun events, and uplifting friendships.

South of Burleigh, there were no groups creating genuine connection for women — I wanted to change that. Living with low vision myself, I know how important it is to feel safe, secure, and included.

What started as a small walking group has grown into a vibrant community of women who show up for themselves and each other. We are more than just a social group — we are a community.
