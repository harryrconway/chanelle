Welcome to
She's Social!

She's Social is a women's community group located on the Gold Coast bringing women together through genuine connection, fun events, and uplifting friendships.

Our
Journey

She's Social began because I noticed something was missing. South of Burleigh, there were no groups creating genuine connection for women. I wanted to change that.

Living with low vision myself, I know how important it is to feel safe, secure, and included. I created She's Social to be a space that celebrates diversity, where every woman feels welcome and supported no matter her background or circumstances.

What started as a small walking group has grown into a vibrant community of women who show up for themselves and each other, building friendships, and unforgettable memories along the way.
